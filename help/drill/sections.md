# Sections

Before creating sections, you must have [processed your drill holes](help://drill/drill.md).

![A drill section](help://drill/image/Section-1-1.png)

Sections are generated by defining start and end coordinates, a width and which layers to include in the section. Sections are transformed to a new coordinate system based on distance along the section from the start point and the real elevation, as calculated in the **Desurvey** stage. This means that your sections are displayed on the same map canvas as the rest of your data. Usually this will be in a different part of space to your normal, plan based spatial data and won't be a problem. If your regular data uses coordinates near 0E, 0N, you might want to move it all under a single group in the Layers panel so that you can switch it on and off easily. All sections are placed in their own **Sections** group automatically.

![Section groups in the Layers panel](help://drill/image/SectionGroup-1.png)

Geoscience stores all the metadata required to recreate a section in your QGIS project file. Generated section layers are only **Memory Layers** and are lost when QGIS is closed, however they can easily be regenerated when you open your project again. Similarly, you can remove sections from the Layers panel and regenerate them later. The only way to delete sections permanently is via the Section Manager.

## Section Manager

![Section Manager](help://drill/image/SectionManagerDlg-1.png)

The **Section Manager** displays all sections in the box on the left. Double click sections in the box to display them. The **Sections** group in the Layers panel is also mutually exclusive, so you can switch between sections by clicking their groups on and off.

You can **Recreate** or **Delete** multiple sections by selecting their check boxes before clicking the appropriate buttons. When you recreate a section, Geoscience replaces any existing empty Memory Layers already in the section's group. This allows you to have extra layers, such as [interpretation layers](#section-interpretation), under the section group. The Geoscience section layers are just recreated around them.

Geoscience creates a top level group called **Sections**. All sections you create are placed under this in their own groups, so that you can switch entire sections on and off.

Sections are normal QGIS layers. You can use any of the symbology and labelling tools as normal. You can also create a Layout to produce scaled and annotated plots.

Geoscience generates three grids and a border with every section. These are placed in a **Decorations** group so that they can be toggled on and off together. The three grids show where lines of Easting, Northing and Elevation cross the section line. If your section is close to west-east or south-north, one of the grids is turned off by default. You can turn any of the three grids or the border on or off independently.

## Creating a Section

1. When a section is created, Geoscience copies the style from your downhole data layers to the new section. Make sure you are happy with your downhole data styles before generating sections. You can change the downhole data style later, then regenerate the sections to update their styles, but it's better to get it right the first time.
2. Open the **Section Manager** by choosing **Geoscience > Drilling > Section Manager...** or click the ![Section Manager](help://../icon/DrillSection.png) button on the toolbar.
3. Type in your desired **section width**.
4. You have three options to create a section:
    1. **Map Canvas** - Use the mouse to draw a section line on the map canvas.
    2. **West-East** - Type in the Northing plus start and end Easting for the section.
    3. **South-North** - Type in the Easting plus start and end Northing for the section.
5. If Map Canvas is chosen, click and drag on the map to define the end points. Geoscience displays a moving box of the section width to assist you. Your section is defined when you release the mouse button.
6. Once a Map Canvas section is defined, or when you define an orthogonal section, a dialog asks you to select which vector and raster layers to include and to choose a name for the section. Check the boxes next to the desired layers, then click **OK**. Only vector layers with 3D coordinates and single band raster layers are listed.

![Choosing the layers for a section](help://drill/image/MapCanvasDlg-1.png)

It may take some time to generate the section, depending on how many drill holes you have. Once completed, the new section is added to the Section Manager, a new group containing your section is added to the Layers panel and the map canvas zooms to your new section.

## Section Interpretation

You can use the standard QGIS tools to interpret on sections. Create a new vector layer and place it under the section group with the rest of the layers. This way it is turned on and off with all the automatically generated layers.

![Interpretation layer inside a section group](help://drill/image/SectionInterpLayerTree.png) &nbsp; &nbsp;
![Lithology interpreted on a section](help://drill/image/SectionInterp.png)

When you save your QGIS project, your interpretation layers are saved with it. When you reopen the project and recreate your sections, the Geoscience layers replace the empty placeholders and your section is returned to the way you left it.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't delete your section in the Section Manager if you have custom interpretation layers. If you do, you will need to make the section again and move the features in your interpretation layer to match the new section.
