![Geoscience](help://../icon/Geoscience.png)

**Geoscience for QGIS** is a free plugin that adds drill hole and other geoscience tools to QGIS. With it you can:

- Desurvey drill holes
- Display downhole data in plan and on sections
- Convert downhole structural measurements to dip and dip direction
- Create local grid coordinate systems
- Stream your QGIS map into Spatial Integration
- Make white or black transparent on raster images

Install Geoscience for QGIS from **Plugins > Manage and Install Plugins** in QGIS. Its commands are on the **Geoscience** menu and toolbar. Choose **Geoscience > Help** to open these pages.

Want to see your drill holes in full 3D? Give [Spatial Integration](site://web/) a try as well.
